About

One person. One website at a time.

purpleDrop is founder-led. The person you speak with is the person doing the work — there is no handoff to a junior, no account manager in between, and no agency layer on top.

The founder is Akshay Patel. He started purpleDrop after watching the same thing happen to business after business: a website gets built, everyone moves on, and years later the site is still saying what the company used to be — while buyers have moved on to asking AI who to hire.

Why the AI angle

Buyers changed how they choose. Most websites didn’t notice.

Ask yourself two things: when did your website last change — and when did your buyers change how they choose?

For years, a website’s job was to be found on Google and look respectable when someone arrived. That job hasn’t gone away — but a new one arrived in front of it. Today a buyer asks an AI assistant who to hire, gets three names, and calls one of them. The website’s job now starts earlier: it has to give the AI something true, specific and current to describe.

Most websites can’t do that, because they’re years out of date. That’s the whole business: find out what AI says about you, then fix the site until the answer names you.

How we work

Small, senior, and plain-spoken.

  • You talk to the person doing the work. From the first call to the launch.
  • We say what we can and can’t do. Nobody can guarantee a specific position in an AI answer, and we won’t pretend otherwise.
  • Fixed price, in writing, before we start. No hourly billing, no surprise extras.
  • You own it all. The domain in your name, the files yours, and the hosting you choose — with help deciding. No monthly fees, no lock-in.
  • We work with established companies — businesses with a website that’s gone quiet and a reputation worth describing properly. In the US, UK, Canada and Australia.
If you’re wondering whether this is for you: if your website is more than a couple of years old and nobody’s touched it since, it probably is. The free check will tell you in thirty minutes.
