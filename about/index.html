About purpleDrop

A small team that does this one thing properly.

We help established companies get recommended by the AI tools their buyers now consult. Half our job is teaching that this is happening at all — most businesses, and their consultants, and their IT teams, simply don't know yet. The other half is the work itself.

What we believe

Values you can hold us to.

Senior-only, always

The people you meet are the people doing the work. We don't sell a senior name and deliver a junior. It's the easiest promise we make and the one we protect most.

Plain English, no exceptions

If we can't explain what we did in a sentence your whole team understands, we haven't finished. Jargon is how people hide work they can't defend.

Fixed price, one written agreement

The number in your written quote is the number you pay. The scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts — so you always know what's being built and what it costs.

Honest even when it costs us

If AI already describes you well, we'll tell you on the first call and send you on your way. Good work starts with a straight answer.

Technical health matters

An outdated plugin or broken SEO today is the first answer AI gives tomorrow. We also check for outdated libraries, unmaintained dependencies, and SEO gaps — so your website doesn't let you down.

Where we sit

Based in the US. Working wherever your company is.

Most of our work is done remotely, in the open, on your tools. We work across all US time zones — the first call is always worth having face to face if we're nearby.

A note on how we got here

We watched buyers stop searching and start asking.

Every week, decisions that used to start on Google now start inside an AI answer. Companies kept building websites for the old conversation. We built purpleDrop for the new one — and for the companies that are still, quietly, absent from it.

Where we've actually done this

We've been the people making AI work inside companies.

Before purpleDrop, we did this work from the inside — not as a slide deck, but as the person your team asked to "figure out this AI thing." Here's what we've actually built, trained, and run.

Built AI agents, not experiments

Agents that automate competitor analysis, research, and the battle cards sales teams actually use — working tools, not proofs of concept.

Trained sales teams on AI assistants

Emails, research, outreach, call prep — taught until it stuck, with the workflows and prompts written down so nobody forgets.

Automated the call analysis

Turned recorded sales and customer-service calls into CRM updates automatically — competitors, objections, and call stories, pulled out and put to work in discovery and demo scripts.

Audited the software stack

Analyzed which purchased tools were actually used. The duplicates, the overlaps, the platforms nobody opened — and the money that went with them.
