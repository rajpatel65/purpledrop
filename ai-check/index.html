The free AI-readiness check

Are you found by AI now?

Thirty minutes, on a call, with your company on the screen. We ask AI the question your buyers ask — and you watch the answer appear, live.

This is not a report we email you later. It happens in front of you, on the call, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot. You see what each one says about your company, what it says about your competitors, and what it’s missing about you.

What happens on the call

Three things, in thirty minutes

1

What would a buyer ask?

We type the question a buyer would ask — the service you sell, in your area — into the AI assistants your buyers actually use. No setup, no preparation from you.

2

What does the answer say?

You see, live, which companies the AI names — and whether yours is among them. We check your competitors the same way, so you can see the gap in plain sight.

3

What’s missing?

We tell you what’s missing: the words, the proof, or the pages the AI needs before it can describe you. If the answer is already good, we say so — and send you on your way.

The questions the check answers

Evidence for the big questions — not guesses.

The check is how you stop guessing about the questions that matter:

  • Why did you get the site — and is it doing that job? The answer shows you what the site is actually saying about you today.
  • Is AI finding you — and describing you correctly? You watch the answer appear, live, and see whether it names you, a competitor, or nobody at all.
  • Does it work on a phone? We look at the site the way your buyers see it — on the device in their hand.
  • Who are you actually talking to? The buyer’s question, and the answer to it, tells you who the site is speaking to — and who it’s missing.
  • Who owns your website? We check who owns your domain — you, your agency, or the web guy you use — and how easy it would be to move it. A lot of owners find out they don’t own what they paid for.

The check gives you the evidence. What it means for your site, and what to do about it — that detail we go through together on the call.

What you keep

The AI-answer review is yours, either way.

After the call you keep a short written review: what AI says about your company today, what it says about your competitors, and what would change the answer. It’s yours whether we work together or not. There is no obligation, no follow-up pressure, and no charge for the call.

If the AI can’t describe you, your buyers never hear your name. That’s the loss the check makes visible — in thirty minutes, before you spend anything.

What happens next

After the check, three ways forward

  • The answer needs work. We quote you, in writing, for the Fix on the site you have, an update to it, or a full redesign if the old site can’t be saved. The quote is fixed, and it arrives the same day. Whichever it is, the site is yours when we’re done — domain, files, hosting choice.
  • The answer is already good. We tell you so on the call. You keep the review, you keep the knowledge, and we part as friends.

Either way, you’ll know where you stand — which is more than most business owners know about their own website today.
