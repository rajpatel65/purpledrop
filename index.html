Why is comparing options so confusing?Same words, very different things.

Before you compare

Why this is confusing

Go looking for help with this and you’ll hear the same words everywhere — “free check”, “AI visibility”, “audit”. They sound like the same thing. They aren’t. Here’s what those words usually mean, depending on who’s selling.

An instant score

Some tools give you a free number in seconds — a score out of 100. No person looks at your site, and nothing gets fixed. A score tells you something is wrong. It isn’t the answer.

An audit, then a subscription

Some agencies offer a free audit first — then a retainer for ongoing optimisation. The audit is the start of a subscription, not a one-off job. The work never really ends, and neither does the bill.

A redesign, priced like a menu

Some designers sell a new website with AI as a feature, at a published menu price, with an ongoing care plan on top. A new site — whether or not the old one could have been saved.

Same words, different things. A score isn’t an answer. A retainer isn’t a fix. And a menu price doesn’t say what’s included — or whether your site can even be saved. So you can’t compare them, and most business owners don’t try.
