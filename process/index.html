How it works

From the first call to launch — and after.

Six questions, in order — answered together, on the call and as the work goes on. You know what happens at each one and what you decide. Nothing starts before you’ve agreed it in writing.

The six steps at a glance

  1. 1The site’s job
  2. 2What AI says todayYou decide
  3. 3What the site should sayYou approve
  4. 4One fixed priceYou agree in writing
  5. 5Design and buildYou approve before launch
  6. 6You own it

The same six steps as below, with the points where you decide.

  1. 1. Why did you get the site — and is it still doing that job?

    We start with your reason, not our services. On the free check call, you tell us what the site was for — leads, fewer calls, a presence — and we look together at whether it’s still doing it.

  2. 2. What does AI say about you today?

    We ask AI the question your buyers ask — live, on a shared screen — and you watch the answer. Is it finding you? Is it describing you right, or describing a competitor instead? You keep the AI-answer review either way.

  3. 3. What should the site say instead?

    Before any design starts, you approve what the site will say. A website that says the wrong thing, beautifully, is still the wrong website. We go through how that works on the call.

  4. 4. What will it cost, exactly?

    You get one fixed price, in writing, confirmed the same day. No hourly billing, no surprise extras. Work starts only after you’ve agreed it.

  5. 5. What will it look like when it’s done?

    You see the work while it’s happening, not in a big reveal at the end. You approve it before it goes live.

  6. 6. Who owns it when we’re done?

    You do. The domain in your name, the files yours, the hosting you chose. No monthly fees, no lock-in. If you want help later, that’s a new fixed quote, not a subscription.

What you approve, and when

You decide at every step. We don’t proceed without you.

  • After the check: you decide whether there’s anything worth fixing. If not, we part ways — you keep the review.
  • After the message: you approve what the site will say, before a single page is designed.
  • After the quote: you agree the price and the scope, in writing, before any build work starts.
  • Before launch: you see the finished site and approve it before it goes live.

That’s the whole process. If at any point you want to stop, you stop.
