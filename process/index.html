How we work

A story in four acts. You see every step.

Most engagements follow the same arc. Not because we're rigid. Clarity demands structure. You stay informed. You see the movement. You stay in control.

I

Act I

We listen

Thirty minutes. One straight answer. We learn your business, your customers, and what a "win" looks like. You walk away knowing what your site needs — even if we never work together.

II

Act II

We plan the message

Before any design, we agree what the site has to say: what you do, for whom, and why it matters. This is the step most projects skip — and the reason most sites underperform. You approve the message before we design a page.

III

Act III

We design and build

Once scope and price are agreed in writing, we design and build. Mobile first, fast by default, a clear next step on every page. You see the work as it happens — not at the end.

IV

Act IV

We launch and care

We launch, then keep it current: hosting, updates, backups, and small improvements as your business changes. You get a monthly report and a site that stays working.

The rules of the road

Three commitments we make on every engagement.

Agreed on paper before it's built

Every engagement runs on a written scope and a fixed price. What we're building, and what it costs, is agreed in writing before work starts.

Fixed price, in writing

The number on the page is the number you pay. No hourly billing, no "while we're in here" extras.

Senior-only, end to end

The strategist you meet in Act I is the one doing the work in Act III. We don't staff up with juniors to hit a margin.

Clarity wins the shortlist.

Every day, buyers ask an AI assistant who to call. That answer becomes their shortlist. We help you shape it, and keep it strong as the systems change. You get the inquiries. We handle the mechanics.
