What a small-business website actually costs.

Real ranges, what is included, and the hidden costs nobody mentions on the sales call.

Published ranges for a small-business website run from about $1,000 to $10,000, depending on scope. Most serious small-business sites land in the middle of that range. Below that, you are usually buying a template with your logo on it. Above it, you are paying for a brand and a strategy, not just pages.

What the number should include

A proper fixed price covers the message, the design, the build, the mobile version, the contact or booking forms, the basics of search setup, and a handover you can actually use. If any of those are "optional extras", that is a sign the price is not really fixed.

The price on the page should be the price you pay. If it is not, walk away.

The hidden costs nobody mentions

Hosting, a domain, the email account, plugins or apps, stock photography, and the maintenance that keeps it current. Budget roughly 15–20% of the build cost a year for care. A site that is not maintained quietly stops working — not dramatically, just slowly.

The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest site. The most expensive one is rarely the best. Ask what is included, in writing, before you decide.
