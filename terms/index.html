Terms

Terms, in plain words.

These are the terms for purpleDrop’s services. The written quote for your project is the agreement that matters most — it states the scope, the price, and the timeline, and it overrides anything general on this page.

The free check

The AI-readiness check is a free 30-minute call. What we show you on the call is a snapshot of what AI assistants say about your company at that moment — answers change as the AI systems change, and the check is not a guarantee of any future answer.

Quotes

Work starts only after you have agreed a fixed price in writing. That price covers the scope in the written quote. If the scope changes, we agree the change — and any change to the price — in writing first. We don’t bill by the hour.

What we guarantee — and what we don’t

We guarantee the work described in your written quote, at the price in that quote, delivered to the timeline in that quote. We do not guarantee a specific position in an AI answer or in search results. Nobody can guarantee that, and anyone who offers you one is guessing with your money.

Ownership and hosting

The website files are yours. You own them outright, and you can take them to another provider at any time. Your domain is registered in your name. You choose the hosting platform, with our help deciding, and we set the site up on the one you pick. There are no monthly fees from us and no lock-in: if you want changes later, they are quoted separately, in writing, before any work starts.

Liability

Our liability for any claim is limited to the fees you have paid us for the work in question. Nothing in these terms limits liability that can’t be limited by law.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the U.S. state where purpleDrop is registered, and any dispute will be handled in the courts of that state.
