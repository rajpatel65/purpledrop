Services

The check first. Then one of three ways forward.

After the free AI-readiness check, the fix is one of three things: a fix on the site you have, an update to the site you have, or a full redesign. All three are quoted in writing, at a fixed price, before any work starts — and whichever you choose, the site is yours when we’re done: the domain, the files, and the hosting choice.

Which one you need is the answer to one question: what does your strategy need now? Pick the one that fits — or don’t pick at all. The check tells you which one you need.

Service one

The Fix — make AI find you, on the site you have

For owners whose site is broadly fine, but AI can’t describe the company. The problem is what the site says and how it says it. We fix that on the site you already have, without rebuilding it.

What changes for you

When a buyer asks AI who to hire, your company is described correctly and named. You see the before and after in writing. What exactly gets done, we cover on the call.

Quoted in writing · Fixed price

Pick the Fix if: your site is broadly fine, but AI can’t describe it. The problem is not the design.

Service two

The Revive — update the site you have

For sites with good bones that have gone stale. We keep what works, fix what doesn’t, and rewrite the message so AI and your buyers can finally describe what you do. Best for a small to mid-sized site with no online store.

What changes for you

A site that looks current, says clearly what you do, and gives a buyer an obvious next step. You keep what already works. What gets updated, we cover on the call.

Quoted in writing · Fixed price

Pick the Revive if: your site looks dated but the structure is sound, and the main problem is what it says — not what it is.

Service three

The Rebuild — start over, properly

For sites that can’t be saved by an update, including larger sites, online stores and anything with backend work. We start from the message: what you do, who it’s for, and why a buyer should call you. You approve that before anything is designed.

What changes for you

A site built around a message you have approved, that you can run yourself afterwards. Nothing you have earned gets lost on the way. How we do it, we cover on the call.

Scoped and quoted on the call · Fixed price in writing

Pick the Rebuild if: the site is broken, embarrassing, or built on something nobody can update anymore — or if the message itself needs rethinking from scratch. Every rebuild is a different size, so the price is scoped on the call, not guessed from a list.

Yours, when we’re done

Ownership — the site is yours, not ours

Whichever of the three you choose, the ending is the same: you own it. No monthly fees, no lock-in, no “maintaining a connection” — and nobody holding your domain hostage.

What you own

  • Your domain, registered in your name
  • The website files, outright. Take them to anyone, any time
  • The hosting choice, made by you
  • No monthly fees. No lock-in. Later help is a new fixed quote, not a subscription

Nothing to cancel, ever

About hosting, plainly: you choose the platform, and we help you decide. If your agency owns your domain today, sorting that out is the first thing we do on the call — before any work starts.

Not sure who owns yours? Check who your domain is registered to (ICANN Lookup, opens in a new tab) — the official lookup, free, no signup, and it takes about 30 seconds.

How the quote works

One number, in writing, before we start.

After the free check, you get one fixed price, in writing, confirmed the same day. You decide whether to go ahead. No hourly billing. No surprise extras. If the scope changes later, you get a new quote in writing first.
