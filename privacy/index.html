Privacy

Privacy, in plain words.

Here is what we collect, what happens to it, and how to ask us to delete it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, company, what you want to discuss, your preferred time, and any message you add. We use a short email-code check to confirm your address is real before your request is sent. Separately, we see simple visit numbers, such as which pages are viewed and how fast they load. Those numbers are not tied to anything you send us through the form.

What we do with it

The details you send through the form are used for one thing: to reply to your request and arrange your call. We don’t sell it, share it with anyone else, or add you to a mailing list. If we quote you for work, your details are kept as part of that quote; otherwise they are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Cookies and tracking

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Cloudflare says it does not use cookies or local storage for this, and it does not track you across sites. Like any website, your browser’s request reaches Cloudflare’s servers, and Cloudflare says it does not keep your IP address for analytics. We don’t run advertising trackers or social-media buttons. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may set a security cookie to protect the site, and the contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile (a bot check) when you send a code. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

To see, correct, or delete the details you sent us, email info@purpledrop.com and we’ll handle it.
