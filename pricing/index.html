Pricing

Quoted in writing. Fixed price. That’s the whole pricing page.

There are no prices on this page, and that’s deliberate. No two websites are alike — a published price would be a guess, and a guess is either too high for you or too low to do the job well.

So instead of a price list, here is exactly how pricing works. But the price is the last question, not the first — the first is whether the site is doing its job. That’s what the free check is for.

How quoting works

One number, agreed before we start

  1. The free check

    We show you what AI says about your company today, live on a 30-minute call. You see the gap before you spend anything.

  2. We agree what you need

    We talk it through on the call. You decide what goes ahead.

  3. You get one fixed price, in writing

    On the call, confirmed in writing the same day. That number is the price. Work starts only after you’ve agreed it.

What the price covers

The quote says exactly what you get.

Each quote is written for your site, and it lists what the price covers. We go through it with you on the call, so you know what you are agreeing to before you agree. Anything outside it gets its own quote, in writing, before any work starts. Nothing else is extra.

The promise, in plain words

Fixed means fixed.

  • Fixed price, agreed in writing before work starts.
  • No hourly billing. The clock never runs on your money.
  • No surprise extras. If it isn’t in the written quote, it isn’t on the invoice.
  • No monthly fees. No lock-in. The project is a project — not a subscription.
  • You own the domain, the files, and the hosting choice. The site is yours, whatever happens next.

And that’s why there’s no menu of prices on this page. A menu price can’t say whether your site can be saved, what it actually needs, or what it would cost — not until someone has looked at it live. That’s what the check is for: the quote comes after it, in writing, fixed. If you’re comparing options and finding it hard to tell them apart, here’s why it’s confusing.

And one honest limit: nobody can guarantee a specific position in an AI answer. If someone offers you one, they’re guessing with your money. What we guarantee is the work, the price, and that the site will be built so AI can describe it.

When do I see the number?

On the free check call, confirmed in writing the same day. You decide in your own time after that.

What if I change my mind mid-build?

Then we stop and agree the change in writing first — including any change to the price. Nothing proceeds on a verbal promise.

Who chooses the hosting?

You do. You own the domain and the files, and we help you decide. The details we cover on the call.

The only way to get a price is the check.

It’s free, it’s thirty minutes, and the number you get afterwards is real — based on your site, not a price list.
