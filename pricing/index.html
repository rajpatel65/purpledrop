Questions

Before you decide.

What exactly do I get?+

A live website, the files, and a short handover note. For the Business Site and above, a 30-minute handover call. The scope is agreed in writing before we start.

How long does it take?+

The Starter is two weeks. The Business Site is three to five. The Redesign is four to eight. We agree the timeline in writing before we start.

Do I own the site?+

Yes. The files are yours. If we host it under the Care Plan, you can take it elsewhere — we'll help with the move.

Do you write the copy?+

We agree the message in writing first (Act II). If you want full copywriting, that's an add-on quoted in writing first.

Do I have to redo my whole site?+

Not always. If a refresh is enough, we'll say so. If a rebuild is genuinely better value, we'll show you why — in writing.

What happens after launch?+

The site is yours. If you want us to host and maintain it, the Care Plan covers updates, backups, security monitoring, and small edits for a fixed monthly fee, cancel anytime.

How much does a website cost in 2026?+

For a small business, published ranges run from about $1,000 to $10,000 depending on scope; most serious small-business sites land in the middle of that range. We quote a fixed fee in writing before work starts.

Who will we actually work with?+

The person you speak with is the person doing the work. No juniors learning on your project. That is a commitment we state on every page.
